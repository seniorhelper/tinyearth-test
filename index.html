Our mission

Made with heart

I'm a grown-up who never stopped being a kid. Lap Planet is the game I always wanted: a whole planet small enough to sit on your lap. I built it with my own kids in mind, and yours too.

Learn by doing

Poke a volcano. Push a tornado around. Fold and fly ten paper planes. Mix colors, grow a garden, and find real constellations in a true night sky. Some of this Earth is real and some is pretend, and both are yours to explore.

Fun first

Learning sticks when it's fun, so every lesson here is something you do with your own two hands.

A reason to come back

Your garden keeps growing while you're away, every badge you earn lifts your rank, and there is always one more corner of the world to find.

What we cheer

Effort, curiosity and kindness. Every battle is pretend. We stand for peace, and for the real Earth we all share.

For families and players ages 10 and up. Have fun!

Privacy

Lap Planet saves your progress only on this headset. If you choose Play with friends, your head and hand positions and your game moves pass through our relay so your friends can see you. Nothing is stored, there are no accounts, no names and no chat. Rated for ages 10 and up.
